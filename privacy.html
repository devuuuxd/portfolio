Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 20, 2026 · Last Updated: October 20, 2026

1. What This Policy Covers

This page explains what information may be processed when you visit my developer portfolio or contact me through its links. The site is a static portfolio hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no visitor accounts, comment system, or form that submits information to me. The current site code also contains no first-party analytics or advertising script.

2. Theme Preference

The theme button remembers your light or dark preference using your browser's localStorage. The stored key is theme, and its value is light or dark. The site reads that value when a page loads so it can keep your chosen theme.

This is browser storage, not a cookie. The site's own scripts do not send the saved theme value to a server. You can remove it by clearing this site's stored data in your browser.

3. Hosting and Technical Information

GitHub Pages hosts this website. Like other hosting providers, GitHub may process technical information about requests to the site, including IP addresses and website usage information, to deliver, maintain, troubleshoot, and protect its services. This information is handled by GitHub under its own policies, not stored in a visitor database that I operate.

I do not receive raw server logs through the pages of this portfolio. For more information, read the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

4. External Fonts, Icons, and Badges

Some parts of the site load resources from other services. Google Fonts supplies the Inter and Noto Sans JP font files. The Coré page loads technology icons from SkillIcons, and the Contact page loads badge images from Shields.io.

When your browser requests one of these resources, the provider may receive ordinary connection information, such as your IP address and browser or request details. I do not operate these services or control their data practices. You can read Google's Privacy Policy and the Shields.io Privacy Policy. SkillIcons is linked from the SkillIcons website.

5. External Links and Messages

The portfolio links to services such as GitHub, LinkedIn, Discord, Spotify, YouTube, Fiverr, and Bento, as well as public project repositories. Following a link takes you to a service I do not operate. That service's own terms and privacy policy apply to what happens there.

If you choose to message me through one of those platforms, I will see the information you send and may use it to reply or discuss your enquiry. The platform's own systems and retention settings also apply to that conversation.

6. Storage and Retention

The site does not have a backend or database for visitor accounts or submitted enquiries. The theme preference remains in your browser until you clear it or your browser removes it. Messages sent through external platforms are handled and retained according to those platforms' practices; I cannot control their records or retention periods.

7. Privacy Questions and Requests

You can clear the saved theme preference through your browser settings. If you have a question about this policy or information you sent me directly, contact me through one of the channels on the Contact page. I can address information within my control, but requests about records held by GitHub or another external service need to be directed to that provider.

8. Changes to This Policy

I may update this page if the website or its data practices change. The date at the top indicates when this version took effect and when it was last revised.
